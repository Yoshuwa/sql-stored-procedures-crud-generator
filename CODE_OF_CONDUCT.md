# Community Code of Conduct

We want SQL Stored Procedures CRUD Generator to be a welcoming open-source project for everyone,
regardless of experience level, identity, background, or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing ideas and code.
- Give actionable feedback and accept feedback gracefully.
- Credit the work and ideas of other contributors.
- Protect private information and never post real credentials or customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized
conduct, or sustained disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that
violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a ban.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security contact or another private channel designated by the maintainers.
